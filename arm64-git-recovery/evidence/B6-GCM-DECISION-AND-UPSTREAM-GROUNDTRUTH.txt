WE ARE NOT FIRST TO ARM64 MinGit AND MUST NOT CLAIM TO BE.
Upstream Git-for-Windows ALREADY ships MinGit-2.55.0.5-busybox-arm64.zip (33 MB, 403 files,
busybox-minimal, arm64), built from MSYS2 clangarm64 packages. That artefact class EXISTS.

THEREFORE OUR CLAIM IS A TOOLCHAIN CLAIM, NOT A NOVELTY CLAIM:
  NOT  "ARM64 MinGit is now possible."
  YES  "The WoA GCC toolchain, running NATIVELY ON ARM64, can PRODUCE an ARM64 MinGit."
This mirrors the MSYS2 clangarm64 git.exe distinction: a prebuilt binary that runs proves the
TARGET; only OUR chain building it proves OUR CHAIN. A precise claim survives checking; an
overreaching one collapses. The value here is that ONE native ARM64 GCC produced the entire
chain (closure leaves + git.exe + curl/openssl/expat/zlib/pcre2), not that the artefact is new.

The upstream artefact is a GOLDMINE precisely BECAUSE it exists: it is ground truth for layout,
content and capability, and it INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED our derived cert-bundle placement at
clangarm64/etc/ssl/certs/ca-bundle.crt (derived before we saw the reference — external
confirmation of a derivation, the strongest form available).
